About CadenceX

Every great brand moment deserves a flawless rhythm.

A product finally ready for the world. A stall at the trade show the team has been preparing for all year. A conference where the right people are in one room.

We started CadenceX because we have seen how much goes into those moments — and how often the experience doesn't match the effort behind it. We're an exhibition and corporate event company that helps brands stand out on the show floor and beyond.

Based in Delhi. Working Pan India.

Creative stall design, precise fabrication and seamless on-ground execution — so you can focus on your visitors, your leads and your brand.
